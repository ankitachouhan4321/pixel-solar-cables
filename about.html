Our Manufacturing Process

Copper Wire Drawing

High-purity copper rods are drawn into precise wire sizes to ensure excellent conductivity and flexibility.

Stranding Process

Multiple copper strands are twisted together to improve strength, flexibility, and electrical performance.

Insulation Extrusion

Premium XLPO/XLPE insulation is applied to provide UV resistance, weather protection, and electrical safety.

Quality Testing

Every cable undergoes rigorous testing for conductivity, insulation, tensile strength, and durability to meet industry standards.

Packaging & Dispatch

Finished cables are carefully wound, packed, labeled, and dispatched to ensure safe transportation and timely delivery.
