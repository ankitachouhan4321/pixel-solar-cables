PIXEL ZHFR Cables

PIXEL ZHFR Cables (Zero Halogen Flame Retardant) are specially designed to provide maximum fire safety by minimizing smoke and eliminating the emission of toxic halogen gases during fire. Manufactured with high-purity copper conductors and premium ZHFR insulation, these cables ensure reliable electrical performance, long service life, and enhanced safety for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

Key Features
  • High-Purity Electrolytic Copper Conductor
  • Zero Halogen Flame Retardant Insulation
  • Low Smoke Emission During Fire
  • Non-Toxic & Low Corrosive Gas Emission
  • Excellent Heat Resistance
  • Superior Electrical Conductivity
  • Durable & Long Service Life
  • Safe and Easy Installation
Applications
  • High-Rise Residential Buildings
  • Commercial Complexes
  • Hospitals & Healthcare Facilities
  • Schools & Educational Institutions
  • Hotels & Shopping Malls
  • Industrial Plants
  • Airports & Metro Stations
  • Public Infrastructure Projects
